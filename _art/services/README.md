The illustrations on /services/, drawn as HTML. Jekyll skips this folder.

To redraw one, render it at 640 × 440 with a device scale of 2 (1280 × 880) in headless Chrome and copy the
PNG to `assets/img/services/` (finance.png, biomed.png, security.png from cyber.html). The fonts come from
Google Fonts, so the renderer needs the network.
